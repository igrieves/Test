# Design package: Radius Web Design

Tier 1, single journey. Concept A, The Growing Ring.

## Brand premise

Radius is the line from a centre outward. A growth ring is a radius made
visible. Nineteen years, 450 sites, one Cheltenham phone number that still
gets answered. The site's whole argument: we are still here, you own
everything, and the price is the price.

## Audience and action

Local Gloucestershire businesses. ONE call to action: call +44 1242 462003.
Every section earns the next scroll toward that number.

## Buyer language (from real reviews and UK business forums)

- Held hostage: domain registered in the designer's name, site on their host.
- Ghosted: asked three times over two days, no reply, does not take calls.
- Creeping bills: hosting and plugin renewals up and up, upsell for SSL.
- Cannot edit own words: GBP 50 to 100 to change one paragraph.

## Signature element

The ring. Concentric SVG arcs that draw on scroll. Section markers as ring
segments. The 19 years shown as 19 rings, not a numeral.

## Hero beat map (6s scrub)

  0.0 - 1.5   macro grain, raking light      "Nineteen years of steady work."
  1.5 - 3.5   camera pulls back, rings form  "Four hundred and fifty websites."
  3.5 - 5.0   rings resolve, centred         "Still answering the phone."
  5.0 - 6.0   settle, wide and still         name + number, CTA

Action lane: centre of frame. Captions live in the upper third and lower
sixth, clear of the rings.

## Below the fold

  1. The four fears, answered flat (hostage / ghosting / bills / edits)
  2. What you get, honest pricing
  3. The work, 450 projects, selected
  4. How it goes, three steps
  5. FAQ, written against the real objections above
  6. Call. One number, tap to dial.
  7. Footer, discloses imagery is AI generated

## Form

No backend. Primary action is a tel: link, so the phone carries the leads.
Secondary is a mailto fallback. Nothing pretends to submit anywhere.
