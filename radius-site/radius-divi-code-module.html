Questions we actually get

The awkward ones, answered.

My last designer put the domain in their own name. Can you sort it out?
Usually, yes. We have done it plenty of times. Ring us with the domain name and we will tell you what is involved before you commit to anything.
What happens if I want to leave?
You take the site and the domain and go. We will hand over the files and help the new person get set up. No exit fee, no holding anything back.
What does it cost to keep running each year?
We tell you the yearly figure at the quote, not at renewal. It covers hosting, the domain and keeping the thing updated. Ask us for the number before you decide.
Can I change the text myself?
Yes. We set it up so you can edit your own words, and we will show you how in about ten minutes. If you would rather ring us instead, that is fine too.
How long does a site take?
Most small business sites take a few weeks once we have your words and pictures. The waiting is almost always for content, so we will chase you gently.
Do you only work with Cheltenham businesses?
Most of our work is Cheltenham, Gloucester and the villages around them, because people like being able to drive over. We do work further afield as well.
