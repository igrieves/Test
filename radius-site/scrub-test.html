Scrub test

Checks whether a video seeks fast enough to be scrubbed by scroll. Nothing is uploaded. This all runs inside your own browser.

Drag the slider. It should follow your finger with no lag and no freezing.

—Duration
—Resolution
—File size
—Avg seek

What this measures: how long the browser takes to jump to a random point in the video. Scroll scrubbing does that constantly, so slow seeks are what makes a scroll hero feel like it is lurching. The usual cause is too much space between keyframes, and the fix is the re-encode command in INSTALL.md. These thresholds are practical rules of thumb, not a standard.
